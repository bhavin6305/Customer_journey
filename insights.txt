CUSTOMER JOURNEY INTELLIGENCE & ATTRIBUTION ENGINE - KEY INSIGHTS

1. Funnel Drop-Off Analysis
A significant percentage of users drop between product view and add-to-cart stage, indicating friction in purchase intent or product attractiveness.

2. Channel Performance
Paid and organic traffic channels show different conversion behaviors. Some channels bring high traffic but low purchases, while others generate fewer but higher-quality users.

3. Customer Segmentation
Returning users consistently show higher purchase conversion compared to new users, highlighting the importance of retention strategies.

4. Product-Level Performance
Certain product categories dominate purchase activity, helping identify high-performing inventory and marketing priorities.

5. Attribution Insights
First-touch attribution identifies which channels bring users into the ecosystem, while last-touch attribution highlights channels that close conversions.

6. Marketing Optimization Opportunity
Channels with low conversion but high traffic volume may require campaign optimization, landing page improvements, or audience targeting refinement.

7. Data-Driven Decision Making
The project demonstrates how behavioral event data can be transformed into business intelligence for marketing, customer experience, and conversion optimization.
